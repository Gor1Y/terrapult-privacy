Privacy Policy for Terrapult

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Developer: islanode studios

Android package: com.islanode.terrapult

Privacy contact: contactislanode@gmail.com

1. About this policy

islanode studios develops and publishes Terrapult (the "Game"). This policy explains how information is handled when you play the Game, including information processed by the advertising provider.

2. Information handled by the Game

We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other direct personal contact information to play Terrapult.

Game progress, settings, and similar gameplay information are stored locally on your device.

Terrapult uses Unity Ads. While we do not directly ask players for personal information, the Unity Ads SDK processes certain information as described below.

3. Unity Ads and advertising data

Terrapult displays advertisements provided by Unity Ads, a service of Unity. Unity Ads processes data to deliver, measure, maintain, and protect advertising services.

Depending on the device, privacy choices, and applicable law, this information can include:

  • Device and advertising identifiers, where permitted.
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from network information.
  • Device characteristics such as operating system, language, and device model.
  • Advertising interactions, such as impressions, clicks, and rewarded ad completion.
  • Diagnostic and technical information related to ad delivery and performance.

Unity processes this information under its own privacy practices. Learn more in the Unity Privacy Policy.

4. How information is used and shared

Local gameplay information is used to maintain game progress and preferences.

Advertising-related information is processed by Unity Ads to display ads, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and support the advertising service.

Information processed by Unity Ads may be shared with its advertising and service partners as described in Unity's privacy policy.

We do not sell player contact information.

5. Children and families

Terrapult is intended for players aged 6 and older, including children.

We do not ask children to provide their names, email addresses, or other contact details to play.

Advertising shown to children is subject to applicable child privacy requirements and Google Play's Families policies.

Parents or guardians can contact us with privacy questions using the email address below.

6. Privacy choices and data requests

Players can manage available device-level advertising privacy settings through their device settings.

Unity provides information about advertising privacy choices and data rights in its privacy policy.

For questions or requests related to Terrapult, contact us at contactislanode@gmail.com.

7. Data retention and security

Local game progress remains on your device until it is removed through available game or device controls, such as clearing app data or uninstalling the Game.

Advertising-related information is retained and protected according to Unity's applicable policies.

We take reasonable measures to limit unnecessary access to information under our control.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the Game, advertising services, or legal requirements.

The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact islanode studios at contactislanode@gmail.com.
